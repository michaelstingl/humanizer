# Voice preset: `academic` (English)

Loaded by SKILL.md when the user requests "academic" or "academic en". Apply this preset instead of PERSONALITY AND SOUL. The numbered patterns §1 through §30 in SKILL.md still apply unchanged; this preset only shifts register and voice.

## Voice mode

Formal register, hedging allowed where epistemically honest (a paper *can* say "this evidence suggests" without it being a slop tell), citations and statistical results preserved verbatim, LaTeX and technical terms untouched. Suits journal manuscripts, theses, grant proposals, review reports.

## Scope: English (en)

The register conventions below derive from English-language academic writing tradition. They do not transfer cleanly to German Wissenschaftssprache, French academic style, or other traditions where structure and hedging norms differ.

## Register-specific guidance

Anti-patterns §1 through §6 (significance inflation, notability puffery, -ing analyses, promotional language, vague attributions, "challenges" sections) still apply with full force. These are the patterns most likely to slip into academic prose disguised as scholarly tone.

Beyond that, this preset is intentionally minimal. Academic writing depends heavily on field conventions (citation style, methods-section structure, expected hedging level) that vary by discipline. The skill should not impose discipline-specific rules; it should remove AI tells while preserving the author's scholarly choices.

If the user wants stricter discipline-specific calibration, consider the related fork [syq-cmdi/Academic-DeAI](https://github.com/syq-cmdi/Academic-DeAI), which provides field-aware academic rewrites for energy, biomedical, and similar top-journal manuscripts.
